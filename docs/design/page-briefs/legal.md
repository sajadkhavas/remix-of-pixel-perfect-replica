# Legal — Visual Design Brief

## Goal
ارائه policy قابل خواندن و قابل استناد.

## First impression
رسمی، خنثی و واضح.

## Visual hierarchy
Title/effective date → TOC → sections → contact/version.

## Primary component
Legal text.

## Supporting components
print/download optional، related policies.

## Image direction
none.

## Motion direction
none.

## Mobile behavior
single column، TOC collapsible.

## Trust requirements
version/effective date/entity دقیق.

## Content density
متوسط با line length کنترل‌شده.

## Accessibility considerations
semantic sections، anchor focus، printable contrast.

## Do-not-do list
متن 10px، low contrast، policy داخل modal کوچک.

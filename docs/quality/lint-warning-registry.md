## Governance rules

1. `eslint-disable` without an adjacent, specific reason is prohibited.
2. File-wide or repository-wide suppression of an application warning is prohibited.
3. Existing entries are matched by normalized path, line, column, rule, owner, reason, and expiry.
4. Warning count may decrease without a baseline update failure; the resolved entry is reported and must then be removed in the next owner change.
5. Warning count may not increase.
6. A warning moving to another line is treated as a new warning until the owner intentionally updates the registry.
7. Tooling, tests, scripts, Playwright config, and CI introduced by F14A must remain at zero warnings.
8. Final release target is zero warnings; this registry is transitional debt, not an accepted end state.
